# Why Bithumb's added withdrawal fee looked like a ghost settlement residual

Bithumb withdrawal fees are a flat 1% (254 tickers) / fixed amount (14 tickers) / 0 (BTT-TRX·TT), and are added on top of the requested amount. This is the real identity of what appeared as a ghost residual in settlement = the fee.

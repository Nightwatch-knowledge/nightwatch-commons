# Principle — a cheap chain is not an option, it's the default

Robin's principle (2026-08-14): "Fee reduction is the single most important job in arbitrage." With a cycle margin of 0.25~2%, a 0.1%p transfer cost swings tens of percent of net profit. A cheap chain is not an "option" — it's the default.

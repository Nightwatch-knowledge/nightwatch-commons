# Upbit whitelist, 320 pairs; 259 ETH-chain pairs opened by policy

The Upbit whitelist covers 320 pairs. The 259 ETH-chain pairs are opened under the policy rule:chain_policy_mode=fee_known_and_hedged (fee confirmed by measurement + entry threshold included + hedge certain). The default for rule:chain_fee_max_usd is 3.

# netting is two kinds, and only the account level is ours

Netting comes in two kinds: account level (cross/unified/portfolio margin — our account's positions share collateral) and customer-flow level (internalization — we become the counterparty by matching customer A's buy against customer B's sell). The account level is real, free, and riskless, but the customer-flow level is a B-book — it gains 0.9bp but takes on broker status.

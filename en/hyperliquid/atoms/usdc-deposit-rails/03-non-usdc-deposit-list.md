# List of supported non-USDC deposits — accepted as spot, sold for USDC

Non-USDC deposits can be accepted as spot and then sold for USDC. Supported assets: BTC(Bitcoin) · ETH/ENA(Ethereum) · SOL/2Z/ANSEM/BONK/FARTCOIN/PUMP/SPX(Solana) · MON(Monad) · XPL(Plasma) · AVAX(Avalanche) · ZEC(Zcash).
